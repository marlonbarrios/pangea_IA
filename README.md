# The Pangea_AI Collective

The **Pangea_AI Collective** (also spelled *Pangea_IA Collective* in some contexts) is an **artistic and research-based initiative** led by **Marlon Barrios Solano** and **María Luisa Angulo** (an artist from El Salvador based in Paris).

It uses the metaphor of **Pangea**, the ancient supercontinent, to imagine and speculate about a **world in constant movement**, **free of essentialisms and borders**. This metaphor becomes an **epistemic and artistic tool**, guiding inquiries into **artificial intelligence**, **decoloniality**, **migration**, and **speculative storytelling**.

---

## 🌍 Core Vision

The collective approaches **AI not just as a technology** but as a **cultural and philosophical space**—a *latent space*—where multiple histories, voices, and epistemologies coexist and interact. It explores how **AI can become a tool for decolonial knowledge-making**, and how **artists from the Global South** can intervene in the infrastructures and imaginaries of machine learning.

---

## 🧭 Main Concepts

- **Post-geographies & Speculative Cartographies**: Rethinking maps, borders, and belonging beyond colonial frameworks.  
- **Chimeric Cognition & Artificial Ecologies**: Investigating AI as an ecological and hybrid intelligence.  
- **Cybernetic Fictions & Governance**: Drawing from historical projects like *Chile’s Project Cybersyn* to imagine alternative futures of governance.  
- **Embodied Resistance & Cultural Memory**: Using somatic, performative, and archival practices to resist techno-authoritarianism.  
- **Protocols & Latent Infrastructures**: Reimagining how digital systems structure knowledge, access, and power.  

---

## 🎭 Activities

The collective operates through **residencies, performances, installations, workshops, and publications** that blend:

- Lecture performances  
- Interactive installations  
- AI-generated art and sound  
- Workshops on AI, cognition, and decolonial practices  
- Open-source archives and digital publications
